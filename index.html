Sarawak will pay up to RM15,000 of your rooftop solar.

On 3 October 2026, Premier Abang Johari said the state will cover about half of a typical RM30,000 home solar system, for every home. Details arrive with Budget Sarawak 2027. Pre-register now and get your roof assessed before the queue forms.

How Sarawak got to RM15,000

  1. 15 Jan 2026

    Gedong Baru: the state offers a 50% solar subsidy (RM15,000 of RM30,000) to buyers of SPK village-expansion homes only.

  2. 25 May 2026

    Sarawak Energy launches its NEM awareness campaign with over RM10 million in subsidies for 2026: RM8,000 to RM12,000 depending on system size.

  3. 17 Jul 2026

    Daro: the Premier confirms subsidised solar as a standard option in Sarawak's affordable housing model.

  4. 3 Oct 2026

    Mukah: the Premier says the state will give up to RM15,000 per house, and encourages every household to install rooftop solar.

  5. 12 Oct 2026

    Budget Sarawak 2027 is tabled. Rules, tiers and timing are expected here.

  6. 2027 (expected)

    Applications are expected to open. Homes that are already assessed and designed can be submitted first.

Questions homeowners are asking

Is the RM15,000 subsidy official?

The Premier announced it publicly on 3 October 2026 in Mukah. The detailed rules haven't been published yet and are expected with Budget Sarawak 2027 on 12 October. We'll update this page the same day.

Should I install now with the 2026 subsidy, or wait for 2027?

It depends on your system size and on whether the new subsidy replaces or tops up the NEM one, which isn't known yet. For small systems the difference may be modest; for a RM30,000 system it could be several thousand ringgit. Pre-register and we'll run both numbers for your home once the rules are out.

Does pre-registering cost anything or commit me?

No. Pre-registration is free and there's no obligation. It puts you on our survey list so your design is ready when applications open.

Who can apply?

For the 2026 NEM subsidy, applications must be filed by a Sarawak Energy registered contractor for a domestic account. Eligibility for 2027 will be confirmed in the official guidelines.

How does Net Energy Metering work in Sarawak?

Your panels generate electricity and Sarawak Energy credits that generation against what your household uses, so you pay only the difference. SESCO installs the metering needed for this.

What about batteries?

Batteries are optional and add cost. The Premier mentioned sodium battery systems in the same speech, but we don't yet know if the subsidy covers them. Ask us for a quote with and without.
